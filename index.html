Account Deletion Request

You can permanently delete your UCI Sarthi account and the personal data associated with it at any time. The process is free of charge.

Email deletion request

Opens your email app with the subject pre-filled — just send it.

1. How to request deletion

Account deletion on UCI Sarthi is processed manually by our team to make sure it is really you making the request.

  1. Email our Grievance Officer at hello@ucisarthi.in using the button above or your own mail app.
  2. Send the email from your registered Google sign-in email address, and mention that you want your account deleted.
  3. We acknowledge your request within 48 hours and may ask a short identity-verification question to confirm you own the account.
  4. Once verified, deletion is completed within 30 days, and you receive a confirmation email.
Identity verification is mandatory. If we cannot verify that the request came from the account owner, we will not proceed — this protects you from unauthorised deletion.

2. What we delete

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your name, email address and profile photo (from Google Sign-In)
  • Your phone number, city and target exam
  • All test attempts, answers, scores and test history
  • Your lesson progress and bookmarked notes
  • All push notification subscriptions
  • Messages you sent through the contact form
  • Your login access — the account can no longer be signed into

Deleted data cannot be recovered. Please be certain before you request deletion — if you have active purchases, consider downloading or completing them first.

3. What we retain and why

A small amount of data is retained after deletion, only where the law requires it or where it cannot identify you:

  • Payment records and order IDs — 7 years. Retained as required under Indian GST and tax laws, so your payments remain auditable.
  • Anonymised, aggregated test statistics — indefinitely. These contain no personal identifiers and are used only for overall course improvement.

Retained data is never shared with third parties.

4. Effect on your account

  • You will no longer be able to sign in to UCI Sarthi with that account.
  • Your paid access is revoked immediately at the time of deletion.
  • Any refund of a recent purchase is handled separately under our Refund Policy — deletion of the account does not forfeit an eligible refund.
  • You may create a new account later, but none of your old data, progress or history can be restored to it.
